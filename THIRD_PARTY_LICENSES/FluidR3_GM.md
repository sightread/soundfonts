# FluidR3_GM attribution and license

The packaged audio samples are derived from the FluidR3_GM General MIDI
SoundFont created by Frank Wen and contributors.

- **Original SoundFont:** `FluidR3_GM.sf2` by Frank Wen
- **Source:** [pianobooster/fluid-soundfont](https://github.com/pianobooster/fluid-soundfont) v3.1
- **Format:** SoundFont 2 (.sf2), rendered to MIDI.js MP3/JavaScript by Sightread
- **Sightread modification:** Repackaged from the SF2 source with checksum
  verification and deterministic release archiving; audio samples are rendered
  per-note via FluidSynth + LAME
