# FluidR3_GM attribution and license

The packaged audio samples are derived from the FluidR3_GM General MIDI
SoundFont created by Frank Wen and contributors.

- Original SoundFont: `FluidR3_GM.sf2`
- Pre-rendered source: [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
- Pinned source commit: `044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef`
- Percussion source: [paulrosen/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/paulrosen/midi-js-soundfonts),
  a fork that adds the General MIDI Channel 10 percussion track
- Pinned percussion commit: `cbd6b6f6d1af89ebfb69402860741288f08ff8b7`
- Upstream format conversion: per-instrument MIDI.js-compatible MP3/JavaScript
  soundfont files
- Sightread modification: checksum verification and packaging into a versioned
  release archive; the audio samples are otherwise unmodified
